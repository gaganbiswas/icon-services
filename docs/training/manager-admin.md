# Manager / administrator

- **Assign technicians** on the Work tab; reassignments are kept in history.
- **Send estimates** (Estimates tab) and share the one-time approval link if the customer is not using the app.
- **Hold / cancel / reopen** jobs with a reason. Delivered jobs reopen only with special permission.
- **Deliver with balance due**: allowed for managers with a written reason (audited).
- **Dashboard** on the home page; **Reports** for registers, technician performance, parts and money.
- **Notification templates**: enter the Meta-approved WhatsApp template names and e-mail subjects.
- **Two-step sign-in**: required for administrators in production. If a colleague loses their phone, an
  administrator with user-management rights can reset it (reason required; they are signed out).
- **Audit log**: who did what and when.
